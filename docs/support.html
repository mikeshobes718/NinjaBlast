Blast Guide Support

Blast Guide is an unofficial iPhone companion for the Ninja Blast portable blender, series BC100BZ. It is not affiliated with SharkNinja.

What you can do in the app

  1. Guide: parts, first-time setup, battery, cleaning, storage, warranty.
  2. Blend: 30 second cycle timer, load order, and the 7 blend steps.
  3. Recipes: the five recipes from the manual.
  4. Lights: match the LED color on the motor base, then tap Call if you need SharkNinja.

The blender itself

This app does not connect to the blender over Bluetooth or WiFi. Use the paper manual and the unit LEDs together with the in-app guide.

For hardware warranty or a jammed unit, use the Call button in the app, or phone SharkNinja at 11 3003-9030 with your receipt.
