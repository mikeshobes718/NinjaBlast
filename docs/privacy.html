Blast Guide Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Blast Guide is an unofficial quick guide for the Ninja Blast portable blender. This page explains what the app does with data.

Who we are

Blast Guide is published by Mike Shobes. Contact: mikeshobes718@gmail.com.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not create an account, does not talk to a server, and does not use analytics, ads, or crash reporters.

The blender guide, recipes, LED colors, and the 30 second timer all stay on your iPhone.

Phone calls

If you tap Call on the Lights or Help screens, iOS Phone opens with SharkNinja Brazil support (11 3003-9030). That is a system phone call. The app does not record the call or send the number anywhere else.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising ID, no tracking across other apps
  • No camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location
  • No login

Children

The app is a kitchen reference. It is fine for anyone who can use a blender with adult help. It does not ask for a name or an email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. The latest version will always be at this URL.
